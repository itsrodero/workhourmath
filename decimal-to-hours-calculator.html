Turn decimal hours from a paystub, timesheet or payroll report back into hours and minutes. 8.6 hours isn't 8 hours 60 minutes — it's 8 hours 36 minutes.

How to convert decimal hours to minutes

Keep the whole number as hours, then multiply the decimal part by 60 to get the minutes.

Minutes = decimal part × 60 7.75 hours → 7 hours + 0.75 × 60 = 7 hours 45 minutes 8.6 hours → 8 hours + 0.6 × 60 = 8 hours 36 minutes

Worked example: a paystub shows 76.35 regular hours for two weeks

  1. Whole hours: 76.
  2. 0.35 × 60 = 21 minutes.
  3. So 76.35 hours is 76 hours 21 minutes — not 76 hours 35 minutes.

Values in between follow the same rule: 0.01 hour = 0.6 minutes (36 seconds), 0.1 hour = 6 minutes.

Why payroll uses decimal hours

Pay is a rate per hour, so it's easiest to multiply by a single number: 7.75 hours × $20 = $155. Hours and minutes like 7:45 have to be converted first. That's why timesheets and paystubs show decimals — and why it's worth converting them back when you want to check your hours against your own records. To go the other way, use the minutes to decimal converter.

Frequently asked questions

What is 0.25 hours in minutes?

15 minutes (0.25 × 60).

What is 0.1 hours in minutes?

6 minutes. That's why tenth-of-an-hour rounding uses 6-minute blocks.

What is 1.2 hours in hours and minutes?

1 hour 12 minutes (0.2 × 60 = 12).

What is 2.5 hours?

2 hours 30 minutes.

What is 0.15 hours?

9 minutes (0.15 × 60).
