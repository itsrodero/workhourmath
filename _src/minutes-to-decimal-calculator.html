How to convert minutes to decimal hours

Divide the minutes by 60 and add the result to the whole hours.

Decimal hours = hours + minutes ÷ 60 7 hr 45 min = 7 + 45 ÷ 60 = 7 + 0.75 = 7.75 hours

Worked example: a 38 hour 20 minute week at $17.00 an hour

  1. 20 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.333…, so the week is 38.33 hours.
  2. Pay: 38.33 × $17.00 = $651.61. (Using the exact fraction, 38⅓ × $17.00 = $651.67 — payroll systems usually round to two decimal places first.)
  3. The common mistake is writing 38.20 hours, which would pay $649.40.

Rounding to tenths or quarter hours

Some payroll systems record time in tenths of an hour (6-minute blocks) or quarter hours (15-minute blocks) instead of exact minutes. Federal rules allow rounding to the nearest 5 minutes, tenth or quarter hour as long as it averages out over time and employees are paid for all the time they actually work.

Frequently asked questions

What is 15 minutes in decimal?

0.25 hours (15 ÷ 60).

What is 30 minutes in decimal?

0.5 hours.

What is 45 minutes in decimal?

0.75 hours.

What is 20 minutes in decimal?

0.33 hours (20 ÷ 60 = 0.333…).

What is 40 minutes in decimal?

0.67 hours (40 ÷ 60 = 0.666…).

How do I convert decimal hours back to minutes?

Multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.6 × 60 = 36 minutes, so 8.6 hours is 8 hours 36 minutes. Use the decimal to hours converter.
