What's in the Excel timesheet

  • Time in, lunch out, lunch in and time out for each day. Leave the lunch columns empty if you don't take an unpaid break.
  • Hours per day in decimal, ready for payroll: 8 hours 15 minutes shows as 8.25.
  • Overnight shifts work: an out time earlier than the in time is counted into the next day.
  • Regular and overtime hours using the federal rule — over 40 hours in a workweek is overtime — and, in the biweekly file, counted separately for each week, as the law requires.
  • Gross pay when you enter an hourly rate: regular hours × rate + overtime hours × rate × 1.5.
  • Dates fill in automatically from the week-starting date, and the sheet prints on one landscape page with signature lines.

The formula behind the hours

If you'd rather build your own spreadsheet, this is the formula the template uses for each day (time in in C, lunch out in D, lunch in in E, time out in F):

=ROUND((MOD(F2-C2,1) - IF(OR(D2="",E2=""),0,MOD(E2-D2,1))) * 24, 2)

Excel stores times as fractions of a day, so subtracting two times gives a fraction; MOD(…,1) handles shifts that pass midnight, and multiplying by 24 turns it into hours. Overtime for the week is =MAX(0, total − 40).

Worked example from the Example tab: 8:45 AM – 5:15 PM with a 12:30–1:00 PM lunch, Monday to Friday, plus Saturday 8:00 AM – 12:30 PM at $18.50 an hour

  1. Each weekday: 8.5 hours at work − 0.5 hours lunch = 8.00 hours; Saturday 4.50 hours.
  2. Total 44.50 hours: 40 regular and 4.50 overtime.
  3. Gross pay: 40 × $18.50 + 4.5 × $27.75 = $864.88 — the same answer as the time card calculator.

Tips for filling in a timesheet

  • Write times as they happen, not from memory at the end of the week. Small daily errors add up.
  • Record unpaid breaks only. Short rest breaks of about 5 to 20 minutes are paid work time under federal rules.
  • Keep a copy. If your paycheck doesn't match, your own record is the starting point. Employers must keep time records too.
  • Check your state's rules. California, Alaska, Nevada and Colorado have daily overtime — see overtime rules by state.

Frequently asked questions

Does the timesheet work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets; the formulas and formatting carry over.

How do I enter times in Excel?

Type them with AM or PM, like 9:00 AM or 5:30 PM, or in 24-hour time like 17:30. The cells are already formatted as times.

Can I change the overtime threshold?

Yes. The "Overtime after" cell is set to 40 hours a week; change it if your employer or contract uses a different number.

Is my name saved when I print?

No. The name field on this page only appears on the printout; it isn't stored or sent anywhere.
