How to convert hourly pay to a salary

Multiply the hourly wage by the hours you work in a year. Full time is usually 40 hours a week for 52 weeks — 2,080 hours.

Yearly pay = hourly wage × hours a week × paid weeks a year Full time = hourly wage × 2,080 Monthly = yearly ÷ 12 Biweekly = yearly ÷ 26 Weekly = hourly wage × hours a week

Worked example: $22.50 an hour, 40 hours a week

  1. Yearly: $22.50 × 2,080 = $46,800.
  2. Monthly: $46,800 ÷ 12 = $3,900. Biweekly: $46,800 ÷ 26 = $1,800.
  3. Quick check: every $1 an hour is about $2,000 a year at full time.

When to change the hours and weeks

  • Unpaid time off: if you take two unpaid weeks a year, use 50 weeks.
  • Part time or variable hours: use your average hours a week. 30 hours at $18 is $28,080 a year.
  • Regular overtime: overtime hours earn 1.5 times your rate, so add them separately with the overtime calculator rather than adding them to the hours here.

Frequently asked questions

$15 an hour is how much a year?

$31,200 a year at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks.

$20 an hour is how much a year?

$41,600 a year full time, or $3,466.67 a month.

$25 an hour is how much a year?

$52,000 a year at 40 hours a week.

How many work hours are in a year?

2,080 is the standard figure (40 × 52). The actual number of weekdays varies a little by year — see how many work hours are in a year.

Is this before or after taxes?

Before. Federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare are taken out of these amounts.
