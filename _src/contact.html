What to include

  • Reporting a wrong result? Send the link to the calculator with your inputs (use the Copy link button) and what you expected to see.
  • Suggesting a calculator? Tell us what you're trying to work out.
  • Spotted an outdated rule? A link to the official source — the Department of Labor or your state labor department — is especially welcome.

We can't give legal advice about a specific job or employer. For a wage dispute, contact the U.S. Department of Labor's Wage and Hour Division or your state labor department.
