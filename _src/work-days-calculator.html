How working days are counted

  1. Count every calendar day from the start date to the end date (including the end date, unless you untick it).
  2. Take out Saturdays and Sundays.
  3. Take out US federal holidays that fall on a weekday, using the day they're observed.
  4. Multiply by the hours in your workday to get work hours.

Your employer may observe a different set of holidays — many private companies close on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas but stay open on the others. If that's you, untick the holiday option and subtract your company's holidays yourself.

When a holiday falls on a Saturday, it's observed on the Friday before; on a Sunday, the Monday after. New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so it's observed on Friday, December 31, 2027.

Working days in a year

A year has 260 or 261 weekdays (262 in a few leap years). Take out the 11 federal holidays and most years have about 250 working days — 2,000 hours at 8 hours a day, compared with the 2,080 hours often used for salary math. See the table in how many work hours are in a year.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between business days and calendar days?

Calendar days count every day. Business days count only Monday to Friday and usually leave out public holidays. "5 business days" from a Thursday ends on the next Thursday.

Does the count include the start date?

Yes. The start date is always counted; the end date is counted unless you untick the option.

How many working days are in a month?

Usually 20 to 23, depending on the month and how many holidays fall in it.
